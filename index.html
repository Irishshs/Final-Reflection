Looking back, I can see the huge change within my development. Who would’ve thought that the once shy and lazy kid would now be making this reflection. It’s quite unbelievable that a person can go through multiple changes across their whole life and that a person cannot remain stagnant. I was the type of kid living as a free bird, I wasn’t the type to make commitments, I wasn’t the type who yearns for an improvement but all of it changed because the fire within me has been lit.

I became the type who seeks competition, I kept comparing myself with others asking myself “if they can, why can’t I?” I’ll always be looking for a way to find the higher standard that I can put on myself and push myself far above the average. So, I guess you can say that I improve through competitions and comparing because if I don’t compare myself with others, I won’t be able to know if I really do improve. That’s why I’m really thankful for my friends who keeps influencing me into a better version of myself, the people who are not afraid to tell me if I’m doing something wrong and are not afraid to correct my mistakes.

Although the current me improves through competitions, I can’t be sure that it will always work out that way. Maybe someday, somehow, I’ll figure out a way to improve myself without the need to compare myself with others. I wish to become the person who keeps striving for improvement, the person who keeps moving no matter how hard the obstacles get. Maybe far in the future I’ll be able to see my own worth without the need to compare, to see that every stone comes in different shapes and sizes where they have their own purpose.
